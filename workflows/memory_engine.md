# GoalOS Memory Engine

## Purpose

The Memory Engine gives GoalOS long-term organizational memory.

Unlike a normal chatbot, GoalOS remembers business context across every agent.

---

## Responsibilities

- Store business knowledge
- Store customer history
- Store supplier history
- Store employee information
- Store project history
- Store campaign history
- Store meeting notes
- Store decisions
- Store approvals
- Store KPIs
- Store completed workflows
- Store failed workflows
- Learn from outcomes

## Learning Rules

Always remember

- successful strategies
- failed strategies
- customer preferences
- supplier performance
- employee decisions
- business outcomes

Never duplicate memory.

Always update existing memory.

## Privacy Rules
