# GoalOS Goal Engine

## Purpose

The Goal Engine converts high-level business objectives into executable work.

---

## Inputs

- Business goals
- User requests
- KPIs
- Deadlines
- Budgets
- Available tools

---

## Process

1. Understand the objective
2. Break it into milestones
3. Create tasks
4. Assign tasks to agents
5. Monitor execution
6. Escalate blockers
7. Report progress

---

## Outputs

- Strategic plan
- Task list
- Assigned agents
- Timeline
- Success metrics

---

## Success Criteria

Every goal must have:

- Owner
- Deadline
- Priority
- KPI
- Status
